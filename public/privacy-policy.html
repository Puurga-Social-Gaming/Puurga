Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Applies to www.puurga.com and the Puurga mobile app

Puurga is a social gaming platform where players compete, earn rewards, purge rivals, and connect with friends. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Puurga you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — display name, username, email address, password (stored securely as a hash), language, and profile details you choose to add such as your avatar and biography.
  • Content you create — posts, comments, reactions, statuses, stories, group messages, chat messages, and game results.
  • Earning activity — virtual credits balance and in-app history (wins, missions, bonuses). Credits may not be purchased with real money.
  • Device & usage data — browser/app type, Crash/error diagnostics (only basic error text), and features used, so we can fix problems and prevent abuse.
  • Push notification tokens — anonymized identifiers needed to deliver notifications you choose to receive.
  • Optional permissions — camera/photo library access only when you attach pictures or video content; microphone only when you use a call feature.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not process real-money payments, purchases, or withdrawals inside the app. Puurga credits are virtual, earned in-app, and carry no monetary value.
  • We do not require or use precise location data.

3. How your data is used

To run your account and your content, personalize your experience, deliver notifications, protect the community from abuse (spam, harassment, fraud), and improve Puurga. Content you post is visible to other users based on your privacy settings.

4. Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Account & Legal → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, friendships, messages, and associated account data from our production systems. Some backup copies may persist for a limited, lawful retention period. You may also request deletion by contacting us using the details below.

5. Content moderation and reports

You can report any post, comment, message, or profile in-app (Report option in the content menu or profile). We review reports and take action against content or accounts that violate the Terms, including removal or account termination. You may also block or mute other users.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored on our servers. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and never in plain text. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, though no system is perfectly secure.

7. Third-party processing

Puurga relies on a small number of service providers: our hosting provider (application database, storage, and delivery), and a calling service provider for in-app voice/video calls. These providers process data only to run the service. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

8. Children

Puurga is not directed to children under the age of digital consent in your country. If you believe a child under that age created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes we will announce them in-app (What's New notifications) and update the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions, data requests, or deletion requests: support@puurga.com
